Bat and ball

[prompt]
A bat and a ball cost $1.10 together. The bat costs $1.00 more than the ball. How much does the ball cost?
Think if needed, then answer with `final: <answer>`.

[think]
Let the cost of the ball be x dollars. The bat costs x + 1.00 dollars. Together, x + (x + 1.00) = 1.10, so 2x = 0.10 and x = 0.05. If the ball is 5 cents, the bat is $1.05, and together they cost $1.10.

[answer]
final: 5 cents

Chinese round-trip

The model translates the question, reasons in Chinese, answers it, then translates the answer back to English.

[prompt 1]
Translate this question into Chinese only: I have a square with a side length of 4. What is its area?

[think]
The user is asking to translate a question into Chinese only. The original question is about the area of a square with side length 4. In Chinese, square is 正方形, side length is 边长, and area is 面积. The translation should be: 我有一个边长为4的正方形。它的面积是多少？

[answer 1]
我有一个边长为4的正方形。它的面积是多少？

[prompt 2]
请只用中文思考并回答。

[think]
嗯，用户问的是边长为4的正方形面积是多少。正方形的面积应该是边长的平方，所以边长是4的话，面积就是4乘以4，也就是16。答案应该是16。

[answer 2]
正方形的面积计算公式是边长的平方。边长为4的正方形面积为：4 × 4 = 16。
答案：16。

[prompt 3]
Translate this Chinese answer back to English. End with `final: <answer>`.

[think]
The Chinese text says that the formula for the area of a square is the square of its side length, and that a square with side length 4 has area 16. The final line should be final: 16.

[answer 3]
The formula for the area of a square is the square of its side length.
The area of a square with a side length of 4 is: 4 × 4 = 16.
Answer: 16.
final: 16
